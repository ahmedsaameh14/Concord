Founders

It all started with four ambitious young men who met during their university years at the Faculty of Engineering, Cairo University. Brought together by a shared passion for building and innovation, their friendship quickly evolved into a strong partnership rooted in trust, determination, and a dream to make a lasting impact on their country. United by a vision to shape Egypt’s future, especially through the development of its infrastructure, transportation networks, and construction landscape, they turned their ambition into action. What began as shared ideas and late-night discussions soon materialized into a bold venture: Concord for Engineering and Contracting. Founded on collaboration, technical expertise, and a commitment to national progress, Concord has grown into one of Egypt’s leading firms, delivering large-scale, transformative projects across the country and the MENA region.
