01 / Company

About Us

Founded in 1989, Concord for Engineering and Contracting is a leading, privately owned Egyptian company, specializing in engineering, procurement, and construction services for large-scale projects in Egypt and the MENA region.

With a proven track record, advanced technical capabilities, and a diversified portfolio, Concord efficiently executes complex projects while fostering long-term progress and sustainability.

Our expertise spans transportation, water and wastewater, construction, and high-end commercial developments, improving the lives of millions.
